Privacy

Sound Matching does not use advertising, sell personal data, or track you across apps and websites.

Most settings are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, sound sets saved in your library and their audio may be stored in your private iCloud account so they can be available on your devices.

When you use the Online Library, Sound Matching uses Apple CloudKit to store the content and information needed to provide that service. This may include published sound sets and audio, titles, descriptions, licence information, hearts, reports, and pseudonymous identifiers. Published sound sets are visible to other users. We do not receive your Apple ID, name, or email address from CloudKit.

Apple processes iCloud and CloudKit data under Apple's Privacy Policy. For questions or requests concerning your data, email soundmatching@unboxingsolutions.com.
